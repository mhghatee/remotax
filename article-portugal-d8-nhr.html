Portugal D8 Nomad Visa & NHR 2026: Tax Incentives & Application Guide

Published by Remotax Research Team • Updated July 2026 • 7 Min Read

Portugal remains one of the top destinations in Europe for digital nomads and foreign contractors. Through the Digital Nomad D8 Visa and updated tax incentive programs, qualifying foreign professionals can live in Lisbon or Porto while benefiting from capped flat tax rates on eligible income.

Key Requirements for the Portugal D8 Nomad Visa

To qualify for Portugal's D8 Digital Nomad Visa in 2026, foreign remote workers must demonstrate:

  • Minimum Monthly Income: At least 4 times the Portuguese minimum wage (~€3,280/month).
  • Proof of Employment: Active employment contract or freelance service agreements with foreign clients outside Portugal.
  • Accommodation & Health Insurance: 12-month lease agreement in Portugal and valid international health coverage.

Tax Benefits: How the 20% Flat Rate Works

Under Portugal's updated talent tax incentive program, eligible foreign remote workers in high-value activities can access a reduced 20% flat personal income tax (IRS) rate on Portuguese-sourced qualified earnings, compared to standard progressive brackets reaching up to 48%.
