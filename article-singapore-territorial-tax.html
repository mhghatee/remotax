Singapore Territorial Tax & Foreign Income Guide 2026

Published by Remotax Research Team • Updated July 2026 • 7 Min Read

Singapore is globally recognized as Asia's premier financial hub due to its transparent territorial tax system. Personal income tax rates are among the lowest in the developed world, capped at 24%, with zero capital gains tax and zero tax on qualifying foreign income.

Key Benefits of Singapore's Tax System

  • Territorial Principle: Foreign-sourced income received in Singapore by individuals is generally exempt from income tax.
  • Low Personal Income Tax Rates: Progressive tax rates start at 0% and cap at 24% for high earners.
  • No Capital Gains Tax: Profits from investments, crypto, or property sales are completely tax-free.
