The Singapore Territorial Tax Framework

Singapore taxes income on a territorial basis. Income earned from employment, business operations, or services performed within Singapore is taxable. Conversely, income earned from outside Singapore is generally completely exempt from Singapore income tax when received by individuals, regardless of whether it is remitted into a local Singapore bank account.

Foreign Income Exemption Under Section 13(7A)

Under Section 13(7A) of the Singapore Income Tax Act, all foreign-sourced income received in Singapore by tax-resident individuals (except where received through a partnership in Singapore) is exempt from taxation. This creates tremendous financial efficiency for location-independent consultants and fund managers holding overseas business assets.

Immigration Pathways: The ONE Pass & Tech.Pass

For top-tier founders and executives, the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass (ONE Pass) offers a 5-year personalized employment visa requiring a fixed monthly salary of at least SGD 30,000 (or outstanding achievements in tech, science, or arts). It provides complete flexibility to operate businesses, consult, and work for multiple entities simultaneously.

Zero Capital Gains & Dividend Taxation

Singapore levies no capital gains tax on stocks, real estate (subject to Seller's Stamp Duty), or digital crypto assets held as capital investments. Furthermore, dividends paid by Singapore tax-resident companies are distributed under a one-tier corporate tax system and are completely tax-free in the hands of shareholders.
