United Kingdom Income Tax & NI 2026: Remote Worker Guide

Published by Remotax Research Team • Updated July 2026 • 7 Min Read

The United Kingdom maintains a transparent HMRC tax framework for employees and self-employed contractors. Understanding how the Personal Allowance (£12,570) and National Insurance (NI) rates affect gross income is crucial for remote workers based in London, Edinburgh, or Manchester.

UK Income Tax Bands (2026)

  • Personal Allowance: 0% on earnings up to £12,570.
  • Basic Rate: 20% on taxable income from £12,571 to £50,270.
  • Higher Rate: 40% on taxable income from £50,271 to £125,140.
  • Additional Rate: 45% on taxable income exceeding £125,140.
