AdSense & Affiliate Disclosure

Last Updated: October 2026 • FTC & AdSense Compliance Guidelines
Mathematical & Editorial Independence
Remotax never alters calculator algorithms, tax formulas, or jurisdiction rankings for commercial compensation. All mathematical outputs are 100% objective and statutory-driven.

1. Purpose and Commercial Transparency Charter

Remotax operates as an open-access, browser-side financial intelligence platform engineered for global remote employees, cross-border contractors, and digital nomads. To maintain our sophisticated taxation calculation engines, continuous statutory monitoring, and daily foreign exchange integration free of charge to all users worldwide, Remotax monetizes through digital display advertising and clearly labeled commercial partnerships.

We believe commercial transparency is a fundamental pillar of user trust. This document outlines how commercial relationships operate on Remotax, how advertising networks utilize data, how we adhere to strict international regulatory frameworks (including the United States Federal Trade Commission 16 CFR Part 255, European Union General Data Protection Regulation, and Google Publisher Policies), and our uncompromising separation between commercial revenue and statutory calculation accuracy.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Remotax participates in Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google LLC ("Google"). Google AdSense utilizes cookies, web beacons, and related tracking technologies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to Remotax and other websites across the internet.

Specifically, Google's use of advertising cookies (historically referred to as the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables Google and its certified partner ad networks to serve targeted, interest-based advertisements to users according to their navigation patterns, geographic indicators, and online interactions.

Users have unconditional rights and tools to manage or disable personalized advertising:

  • Google Ad Settings: You can inspect, customize, or disable personalized advertising delivered by Google by accessing the official Google Ad Settings Manager.
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): You may opt out of third-party behavioral advertising from participating ad networks via the NAI Consumer Opt-Out Tool.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): North American visitors can manage cross-industry ad targeting preferences through the DAA WebChoices Browser Tool.
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA): Visitors residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland can exercise targeted ad preferences via the Your Online Choices Interactive Portal.

Opting out of interest-based advertising does not eliminate advertisements from displaying on Remotax; rather, it ensures that advertisements rendered will be contextual (relevant strictly to the current page topic) rather than personalized to your historical browsing footprint.

3. Third-Party Ad Vendors & Programmatic Header Bidding

In addition to Google AdSense direct demand, Remotax may collaborate with certified Google Certified Publishing Partners (GCPP) and programmatic ad exchanges. These programmatic partners may serve display banners, native recommendation widgets, and interactive ad units across designated advertising containers.

Third-party ad vendors operate under strict contractual obligations requiring compliance with regional data privacy laws, including the European Union's IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). These vendors do not receive access to any private financial figures, income inputs, or calculation queries submitted to our client-side calculators, as all mathematical processing executes strictly in your local browser sandbox.

4. Mathematical Integrity & The Editorial Firewall

Our core computational models—including progressive tax brackets, social security contribution caps, special expatriate tax incentives (e.g., Spain's Beckham Law, Portugal's IFICI regime, Italy's Regime Impatriati, Dubai's 0% Free Zone framework, and Thailand's DTV visa tax treatment)—are authored, verified, and audited entirely independent of commercial advertisers.

We enforce an impenetrable editorial and computational firewall governed by the following immutable principles:

  • Zero Paid Calculation Bias: No government tax authority, immigration law firm, relocation agency, or financial sponsor can purchase favorable net salary outputs, artificial deductions, or altered effective tax rates in our simulation models.
  • Objective Statutory Logic: Tax calculations are derived strictly from published legislation, ministerial decrees, official tax agency schedules (such as Agencia Tributária, Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira, Agenzia delle Entrate, HMRC, and the German Bundesfinanzministerium), and validated European Central Bank foreign exchange rates.
  • Neutral Country Benchmarks: Relocation rankings and tax comparison summaries are generated purely through transparent quantitative metrics (net take-home pay, statutory healthcare access, cost-of-living purchasing power) rather than commercial sponsorship bids.

5. Affiliate Relationships & FTC 16 CFR Part 255 Disclosure

In compliance with the United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines concerning the use of endorsements and testimonials in advertising (16 CFR Part 255), Remotax discloses that certain outbound hyperlinks featured on our platform are affiliate referral links.

When you click on an affiliate link and subsequently purchase a product, open an international banking account, procure international health insurance, or contract relocation consultancy services, Remotax may receive a referral commission or monetary compensation from the merchant partner. This compensation occurs at absolutely no additional cost to you; in many cases, our affiliate partnerships negotiate exclusive discounts or fee waivers for Remotax users.

Our affiliate partner selection criteria require rigorous vetting:

  • We only partner with regulated, reputable financial institutions, certified tax consultancy practices, and established digital nomad service providers.
  • Commercial partnerships never dictate editorial recommendations. If an un-partnered service or open-source tool offers superior utility for remote workers, we prominently present it regardless of monetization opportunities.
  • All affiliate links are clearly denoted, and outbound commercial hyperlinks are tagged with appropriate rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow" attributes in full adherence to Google Search Quality guidelines.

6. User Experience & Non-Intrusive Layout Standards

Remotax maintains rigorous quality standards regarding ad density, placement, and visual layout. We adhere strictly to the Coalition for Better Ads standards and Google Publisher Policies:

  • Zero Layout Shifts (CLS Protection): All ad units are reserved with fixed-dimension CSS bounding boxes to prevent unexpected content jumping, CLS regression, or erratic scrolling behavior during page load.
  • Clear Visual Demarcation: Every commercial unit is clearly labeled with identifiers such as "Advertisement", "Sponsored", or "Promoted Resource" to eliminate ambiguity between editorial content and paid advertisements.
  • No Deceptive Placements: Advertisements are never positioned in a manner that mimics interactive application buttons, calculator sliders, navigation links, or official governmental notices.
  • No Intrusive Modals: We do not deploy disruptive pop-ups, auto-playing video units with sound, countdown interstitial blockers, or screen-obscuring overlays that impede access to statutory calculations.

7. Consent Management & International Data Protection

For visitors accessing Remotax from jurisdictions subject to data privacy legislation—including the European Union / European Economic Area (GDPR), United Kingdom (UK GDPR), and California (CCPA/CPRA)—Remotax integrates a standard Consent Management Platform (CMP). This interface allows you to granularly permit or deny non-essential tracking cookies, personalization cookies, and programmatic ad measurement at any time.

For complete details regarding our data minimization practices, zero-storage mathematical processing, and your statutory privacy rights, please review our comprehensive Privacy Policy.
