Methodology & Logic

Algorithmic Sovereign Matching: How Our Relocation Recommendation Engine Operates

The Remotax Smart Match Engine uses multi-variable algorithmic scoring to map an individual's gross income profile, worker classification, and personal relocation goals against published sovereign immigration and statutory tax frameworks across Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

1. The Four Algorithmic Evaluation Dimensions

Our matching model evaluates four interdependent statutory axes:

  • Gross Earning Tier & Minimum Income Eligibility: National nomad visas impose non-negotiable statutory income floors. For example, remote workers earning under $60,000 are ineligible for Portugal's D8 visa (€3,280/mo required) or Spain's Digital Nomad Visa (€2,646/mo required), but qualify for Thailand's Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) or lower-threshold regional visas.
  • Employment Classification Architecture: Distinguishing between direct employment, Employer of Record (EOR) models, and B2B contracting. Certain expatriate tax regimes (such as Spain's Beckham Law) historically favored employment contracts, whereas other frameworks cater specifically to independent self-employed sole traders.
  • Sovereign Fiscal Optimization Goal: Aligning preferences between pure tax elimination (0% regimes like the UAE), balanced European social democracies offering flat tax rates (20%–24% under IFICI or Beckham Law), or low-cost tropical lifestyle hubs offering territorial tax protections.
  • Schengen & Global Mobility Rights: Evaluating whether the applicant requires European Union freedom of movement or operates effectively under standalone regional multi-entry visas.

2. Cross-Border Tax Risk Evaluation

Our scoring algorithm accounts for secondary legal risks that arise during international relocation:

  • Permanent Establishment (PE) Exposure: Recommending EOR or contractor transition pathways if the applicant's role involves commercial negotiation or contract signature.
  • Controlled Foreign Corporation (CFC) Frictions: Flagging potential domestic attribution issues for workers maintaining single-member LLCs while residing in strict civil law jurisdictions.
  • Social Security Leakage: Calculating whether bilateral totalization agreements (A1 certificates or Certificates of Coverage) exist between origin and destination countries.

3. Pre-Relocation Statutory Verification Checklist

Prior to signing international residential leases or relocating tax residency, remote professionals should complete three mandatory due diligence steps: 1) Secure an official certificate of tax compliance from departure revenue authorities; 2) Confirm that your employment contract contains an explicit cross-border telework authorization clause; and 3) Procure certified sworn translations of criminal background checks, employment letters, and educational credentials.

The engine matches your gross salary against statutory minimum visa income requirements, evaluates your corporate structure (Contractor vs EOR), and weights your stated preference (0% tax vs EU lifestyle vs low cost of living) against verified legal frameworks to recommend the jurisdiction delivering maximum net utility.

Yes. You can retake the quiz as often as you wish by clicking the "Retake Quiz" button. Testing different salary levels reveals how earning progression unlocks higher-tier expatriate regimes such as Spain's Beckham Law or Dubai Free Zone licenses.

No. Consistent with Remotax's strict client-side zero-storage architecture, quiz state is held purely in active browser JavaScript memory. No user selections, salary numbers, or matching outcomes are transmitted to or stored on any remote server.
