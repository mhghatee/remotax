Netherlands 30% Ruling Tax Guide 2026: Expat Salary Breakdown

Published by Remotax Research Team • Updated July 2026 • 7 Min Read

The Netherlands offers a famous tax incentive known as the 30% Ruling. Eligible foreign employees hired from abroad can receive up to 30% of their gross salary completely tax-free as reimbursement for extraterritorial relocation expenses.

Key 30% Ruling Eligibility Criteria

  • Recruited from abroad (>150km from Dutch border).
  • Specific expertise not readily available in the local Dutch labor market.
  • Minimum taxable salary threshold met.
