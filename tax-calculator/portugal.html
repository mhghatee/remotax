Portugal Tax Structure for Remote Workers (2026)

Portugal remains a premier European tech hub, anchored by the D8 Digital Nomad Visa and Madeira International Business Center opportunities. Understanding your tax burden depends heavily on whether your activity qualifies under Article 58-A (IFICI) or standard progressive IRS brackets.

IRS Brackets & Simplified Regime

Under the Regime Simplificado for Category B independent professionals, 75% of your gross professional income is assumed taxable, with 25% deemed statutory business expenses. Standard progressive IRS rates range from 14.5% to 48%.
