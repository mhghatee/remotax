Spain Tax Rules Overview for Remote Workers (2026)

Spain is one of the world's most desirable destinations for location-independent professionals. However, its tax structure distinguishes sharply between standard tax residents and those covered by special expatriate legislation.

Standard Progressive IRPF vs. Beckham Law

Under ordinary Spanish tax residency (spending >183 days or centering economic interests in Spain), worldwide income is taxed progressively across combined state and autonomous community brackets ranging from 19% up to 47% (or 50%+ in regions like Catalonia).

The Beckham Law (Régimen Especial de Trabajadores Desplazados) provides a flat 24% rate on employment and digital nomad income up to €600,000 for up to 6 fiscal years. Income above €600,000 is taxed at 47%.
