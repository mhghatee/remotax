UK Income Tax & National Insurance Rules (2026)

The UK tax system combines progressive income tax brackets (Basic rate 20%, Higher rate 40%, Additional rate 45%) with National Insurance Contributions (NICs). Remote workers operating through an umbrella company or overseas employer of record (EOR) face standard PAYE payroll deductions.

The £100,000 Personal Allowance Taper Trap

For every £2 earned above £100,000, your Personal Allowance of £12,570 is reduced by £1. Combined with the 40% higher rate tax plus 2% employee National Insurance, this creates an effective marginal tax rate of 60% on income between £100,000 and £125,140.
