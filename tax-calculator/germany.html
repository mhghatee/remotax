Germany Tax Rules & Deductions (2026)

Germany operates a progressive income tax system with statutory solidarity surcharge exemptions for standard brackets. Remote workers, tech contractors, and employees benefit from substantial deductions for remote home offices (Homeoffice-Pauschale up to €1,260/year) and work equipment.
