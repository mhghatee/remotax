Dubai 0% Personal Tax Architecture for Expats (2026)

The United Arab Emirates does not levy personal income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on individuals. The Dubai Virtual Working Program allows foreign remote employees and business owners to reside legally in Dubai for one year (renewable) while working for employers or clients outside the UAE.

Corporate Tax vs. Personal Income Tax

While federal Corporate Tax of 9% applies to taxable business profits above AED 375,000, genuine personal employment income and independent freelancer earnings below commercial registration thresholds remain completely exempt from personal tax.
