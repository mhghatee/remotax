Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, authoritative explanations on global remote work taxes, statutory thresholds, residency tests, and expat incentives.

How does the 183-day tax residency rule work?

The 183-day test is the most common statutory threshold used globally. In general, spending more than 183 days in a country within a calendar or rolling 12-month period establishes tax residency. However, it is not the sole test: countries also evaluate whether you maintain a permanent home, your center of vital interests (family, business, investments), and habitual abode. Many bilateral Double Tax Treaties (DTTs) use "tie-breaker" rules to determine residency when two nations claim you as a resident.

Does a Digital Nomad Visa automatically mean zero tax?

No. A visa or residency permit provides the legal authorization to live and work remotely in a country. Tax liability is governed separately by domestic tax statutes and international treaties. While some countries (such as Dubai, Thailand under specific remittance rules, or Greece with a 50% discount) provide specific tax incentives for nomad visa holders, others tax you at ordinary progressive tax rates once you become a resident.

How is Spain's Beckham Law calculated for remote workers?

Spain's Special Expatriate Tax Regime (governed by Article 93 of the IRPF Act) allows qualifying remote workers and founders moving to Spain to be taxed as non-residents on Spanish-source employment income at a flat 24% rate up to €600,000 (47% above €600,000) for the year of arrival plus the next 5 tax years. Foreign investment income is generally exempt from Spanish wealth and income tax under this regime.

What is the difference between B2B Contractor and EOR Employee?

As a B2B contractor, you operate independently and invoice foreign clients directly. You are responsible for registering locally (e.g., Autónomo in Spain, Sole Trader in the UK, 1099 in the US), paying self-employed social security quotas, and filing quarterly VAT and income tax returns. You can usually deduct legitimate business overheads.

Under an Employer of Record (EOR) model, a third-party entity acts as your formal in-country legal employer. They run payroll, withhold mandatory employee income tax and social insurance at source, and provide local labor protections.

How does Dubai / UAE 0% tax work for remote contractors?

The UAE has 0% personal income tax and 0% personal social security withholdings on remote worker earnings under the Dubai Virtual Working Program. However, living in Dubai does not exempt citizens of countries with citizenship-based taxation (such as the United States) from their annual home country reporting requirements (e.g. Form 1040, FBAR, and FATCA), although tools like the Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE) often substantially mitigate tax liabilities.
