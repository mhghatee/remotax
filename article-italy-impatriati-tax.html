Italy Impatriati Tax Regime 2026: 70% Exemption Guide

Published by Remotax Research Team • Updated July 2026 • 7 Min Read

Italy offers one of Europe's most aggressive tax incentives for incoming high-skilled workers and remote entrepreneurs. Under the Regime Impatriati, qualifying individuals moving tax residency to Italy can enjoy a 50% to 70% exemption on taxable employment or self-employed income for up to 5 years (extendable to 10 years).

Key Eligibility Requirements

  • Non-Italian tax resident for the previous 2 consecutive tax years.
  • Commitment to reside in Italy for at least 2 tax years.
  • Work activity performed mainly within Italian territory.
