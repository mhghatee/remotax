Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • GDPR & International Privacy Standards
100% Client-Side Financial Calculation
Your financial figures, salary numbers, expenses, and tax details never leave your local browser session. Remotax operates on a zero-remote-storage principle for calculator inputs.

1. Introduction

Remotax ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website at https://afrix.ir/remotax/. We are deeply committed to safeguarding user confidentiality and protecting individual data rights in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and modern international standards.

2. Local Data Processing Architecture

All financial figures, salaries, deductions, and expenses entered into Remotax calculators are processed entirely on your local device (client-side within JavaScript). We do not collect, store, or transmit your individual income or tax inputs to our servers or any third parties.

3. Cookies & Advertising (Google AdSense)

We partner with third-party advertising networks such as Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive advertisements when you visit our website. Google and its certified partners use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to our site and other destinations across the web.

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ad Settings or via aboutads.info.

4. Web Performance Analytics

We measure aggregated, non-personally identifiable analytical events (such as page rendering latency, browser type, and country calculator usage counts) to optimize engine speed and device compatibility. No sensitive financial parameters are ever attached to analytics events.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions, feedback, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our client-side architecture, please reach out via our official Remotax channels.
