Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • GDPR & International Privacy Standards
100% Client-Side Financial Calculation
Your financial figures, salary numbers, expenses, and tax details never leave your local browser session. Remotax operates on a zero-remote-storage principle for calculator inputs.

1. Data Controller & Policy Scope

Remotax ("we", "our", or "us") operates the global remote work financial intelligence platform accessible at https://afrix.ir/remotax/. This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and protection of information obtained from visitors, subscribers, and users across our calculators, country comparison hubs, salary benchmarks, and educational tax guides.

Remotax operates as the designated Data Controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679 - "GDPR") and the UK Data Protection Act 2018. We are committed to uncompromised privacy preservation, enforcing strict data minimization principles and employing edge client-side calculation sandboxing to ensure your sensitive financial deliberations remain entirely confidential.

2. Technical Architecture: Zero-Remote-Storage Calculation Sandbox

Unlike conventional web-based financial portals that transmit user entries to remote backends for calculation, Remotax is engineered on an innovative Client-Side Zero-Storage Architecture:

  • In-Memory Processing: Every monetary input, salary slider adjustment, expense deduction claim, and worker classification toggle executes exclusively within your web browser's JavaScript runtime engine.
  • Zero Network Transmission: When you calculate net take-home pay, progressive tax brackets, or social security liability for Spain, Portugal, Italy, UAE, Thailand, Germany, or the UK, your numbers are never transmitted via HTTP/HTTPS POST or GET query parameters to our web server.
  • No Server Database Retention: Remotax does not operate user accounts, cloud database records, or centralized profiling databases for calculator entries. Closing your browser tab immediately flushes all active calculation variables from system RAM.

3. Automatically Collected Technical Logs & Edge Metrics

When your browser requests static assets (HTML, CSS, JavaScript modules, SVG icons, and WOFF2 typography files) from Remotax, our web servers and content delivery network (CDN) edge nodes automatically register standard technical HTTP request headers. This technical data includes:

  • Truncated, anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (with the final octet masked to prevent individual geolocational re-identification).
  • HTTP request timestamps, response status codes, and transferred byte volumes.
  • Browser user-agent strings, operating system version, and device viewport categories (desktop vs. mobile) utilized strictly for responsive layout calibration.
  • Referring URL sources (indicating which search engine or inbound link directed you to Remotax).

This technical log data is processed under the lawful basis of Legitimate Interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) to defend against Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) vectors, diagnose server errors, and optimize edge asset delivery latency.

4. Cookies, Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Technologies

Remotax displays digital advertising delivered through Google AdSense and programmatic exchange partners to finance our platform operations. These advertising partners utilize cookies, device identifiers, and web beacons to serve contextually relevant and interest-based promotions.

DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their historical navigation across Remotax and other sites on the World Wide Web. When an advertisement is displayed or clicked, a unique cookie identifier may be placed in the user's browser cache.

Third-Party Vendors: Third-party ad vendors and ad networks participating in the Google Certified Publishing Partner ecosystem may also store and read cookies on your browser, collect IP addresses, and evaluate advertising campaign impressions. These third-party technologies are governed by the respective privacy policies of those independent vendors.

5. Consumer Opt-Out & Advertising Preference Controls

You have full autonomy to control, inspect, or deactivate personalized advertising tracking cookies at any time without impacting your ability to use Remotax calculators:

  • Google Ad Settings: Configure or disable personalized advertising served by Google via the Google Ad Settings Dashboard.
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): Opt out of behavioral ad networks through the NAI Consumer Opt-Out Platform.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): Manage multi-industry tracking preferences at AboutAds.info Choices.
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA): Visitors in the EU/EEA can adjust behavioral advertising settings through the Your Online Choices Interactive Tool.

Additionally, you may instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or notify you whenever a cookie is set. Note that blocking all cookies may impact session preference persistence (such as dark mode toggles or currency selections).

6. European Privacy Rights (GDPR & UK GDPR)

If you reside within the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you possess statutory data protection rights under Articles 15 through 22 of the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): You have the right to request confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is processed and receive a transparent copy.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): You may request immediate correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten" - Art. 17): You may request the deletion of your personal technical log records where no overriding legal retention obligation exists.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18): You may restrict the processing of your data under specific statutory circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): You may obtain your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): You possess an absolute right to object to the processing of personal data for direct marketing and targeted advertising purposes.
  • Automated Decision-Making: Remotax does not subject users to decisions based solely on automated processing or profiling that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at the coordinates listed below. You also hold the right to lodge a formal complaint with a European Data Protection Supervisory Authority (such as the AEPD in Spain, CNIL in France, BfDI in Germany, or the ICO in the United Kingdom).

7. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA & CPRA) Notice

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 ("CCPA/CPRA"), California residents are entitled to specific disclosures regarding personal information collected, sold, or shared:

  • Categories of Data Collected: In the preceding 12 months, Remotax has collected internet and electronic network activity information (such as browser type, operating system, and IP address) via server logs and advertising cookies.
  • Zero Sale of Personal Financial Information: Remotax has never sold, rented, or monetized personal financial inputs, salary data, or calculator usage parameters to data brokers or third parties.
  • Sharing for Cross-Context Behavioral Advertising: Remotax engages with third-party advertising partners (Google AdSense) who may collect pseudonymous cookie identifiers for cross-context behavioral advertising. California consumers have the right to opt out of such sharing via our Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal detection or the ad opt-out links above.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you (by denying service, charging different prices, or diminishing calculator functionality) for exercising your CCPA/CPRA statutory rights.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA Compliance)

Remotax is an informational and financial analysis utility designed for adult working professionals, cross-border contractors, and remote employees. We do not intentionally target, market to, or collect personal information from children under the age of 16. If we become aware that an individual under 16 has submitted personal data without verified parental consent, we will take immediate operational steps to expunge such records from server logs.

9. Cybersecurity & Transport Layer Encryption

We deploy robust organizational and technological safeguards to defend our infrastructure against unauthorized access, exfiltration, or tampering. All communication between your device and Remotax is secured using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with 256-bit cryptographic ciphers, HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) with a one-year max-age, automated Subresource Integrity (SRI) validation, and strict Content Security Policies (CSP).
