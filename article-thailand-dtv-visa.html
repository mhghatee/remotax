Thailand DTV Nomad Visa 2026: 17% Flat Tax & Foreign Income Guide

Published by Remotax Research Team • Updated July 2026 • 6 Min Read

Thailand's new Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) has quickly become one of the world's most popular long-term visas for remote employees, software engineers, and digital entrepreneurs. Valid for 5 years with 180-day stay periods per entry, the DTV offers unparalleled flexibility in Southeast Asia.

DTV Visa Eligibility & Financial Requirements

Applicants must satisfy key financial criteria:

  • Bank Balance Requirement: Proof of at least 500,000 Thai Baht (~$14,000 USD) in savings or liquid assets.
  • Remote Employment Status: Contract with a non-Thai employer or business portfolio demonstrating overseas remote income.
  • 5-Year Validity: Multiple-entry visa allowing continuous stay up to 180 days (extendable for another 180 days).

Tax Implications for DTV Holders

Thailand taxes residents (staying >180 days per calendar year) on foreign-sourced income brought into the country within the same tax year. Under qualifying remote structures, foreign income kept offshore or remitted in subsequent tax years remains tax-free.
