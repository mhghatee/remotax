Remotax Tax & Calculation Methodology 2026

The Remotax financial intelligence platform is designed to provide location-independent remote employees, freelancers, digital nomads, and global mobility teams with transparent, mathematically rigorous cross-border tax and net pay projections. Unlike generic tax estimators that rely on simplified flat averages or outdated brackets, the Remotax engine models complete statutory tax codes, official income tax brackets, mandatory national social security contributions, earnings ceilings, and targeted expatriate regimes for the 2026 tax year. This document details our data verification protocol, core calculation algorithms, modeling assumptions, and statutory limitations.

Data Verification & Statutory Sources Protocol

Every tax table, bracket threshold, standard deduction, and social security rate utilized in Remotax is sourced directly from official government tax authorities and statutory legislative gazettes. Our wave-1 jurisdiction tables are verified against official publications including the Agencia Tributaria and Boletín Oficial del Estado in Spain, the Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira in Portugal, the Federal Tax Authority and Ministry of Finance in the United Arab Emirates, the Agenzia delle Entrate and INPS in Italy, and the Thai Revenue Department in Thailand. Each country model carries explicit data integrity fields: taxTablesStatus, socialSecurityStatus, verifiedOn timestamp, and direct deep-link citations to official documentation.

Core Calculation Engine & Statutory Algorithms

Our calculation engine implements strict statutory computational rules. For salaried employees, gross income is adjusted for mandatory statutory deductions (such as Spain's €2,000 employment expense allowance and Portugal's €4,587.09 specific deduction) before progressive personal income tax brackets are applied. Employee social security contributions are computed according to official statutory rates and capped at national statutory maximum contribution bases (such as Spain's €61,214.40 annual ceiling and Italy's €122,295 INPS massimale). For independent contractors, the engine models real-income contribution tramos (Spain's 15 tramos under RDL 13/2022), simplified regime expense coefficients (Portugal's 75% taxable coefficient under Art. 31 CIRS with 12xIAS ceiling), or dedicated separate schemes (Italy's 26.07% Gestione Separata). In Thailand, our baseline calculation strictly defaults to the remitted income progressive PIT scenario pursuant to Departmental Instructions Por. 161/2566 and Por. 162/2566, presenting the offshore 0% unremitted case strictly as a labelled optional exception.

Cost of Living & Purchasing Power Benchmarks

To deliver realistic disposable income projections, Remotax benchmarks baseline monthly living expenses for a single professional residing in primary metropolitan hubs (Madrid, Lisbon, Dubai, Rome, and Bangkok). Baseline budgets encompass private one-bedroom apartment leasing in central districts, utilities (electricity, water, air conditioning or heating), high-speed telecommunications, grocery shopping, local transit, and basic dining. Disposable net savings are derived by subtracting monthly living costs in EUR from calculated net monthly take-home pay.

Remotax Living Cost Benchmark Specifications & Worker Assumptions

Every living-cost figure published on Remotax is an independent research benchmark modeled for a single expatriate or remote professional, and kept visually distinct from statutory tax figures. The Remotax model establishes the following baseline parameters:

  • Worker Profile: Single individual remote knowledge worker without children or dependents.
  • Housing Accommodation: Private, furnished 1-bedroom apartment in a prime or safe, expat-friendly residential neighborhood of the benchmark city (Madrid Centro/Salamanca, Lisbon Parque das Nações/Saldanha, Milan Porta Nuova/Navigli, Dubai Marina/Downtown, Bangkok Sukhumvit).
  • Utilities & Telecommunications: Electricity, water, central heating/air-conditioning, and uncapped high-speed fiber internet (>= 300 Mbps).
  • Monthly Sustenance & Transit: Monthly grocery basket, 1–2 restaurant meals weekly, municipal transit pass, and basic fitness/coworking access.
  • Dated Baseline: October 2026 benchmark data converted using official European Central Bank reference exchange rates as of October 16, 2026.

Dated Benchmark Foreign Exchange (FX) Protocol

To maintain mathematical consistency across international comparisons and purchasing power calculations, Remotax normalizes cross-border living costs and salary tiers to EUR using dated benchmark exchange rates as of 2026-10-16 (sourced from European Central Bank reference fixings and statutory pegs). The dated benchmark rates are: EUR/USD = 1.1253 (establishing US Dollar conversion), EUR/THB = 37.602 (establishing Bangkok baseline living cost of ฿38,500 = €1,024 monthly), and EUR/AED = 4.1327 (reflecting the statutory peg of 3.6725 AED/USD; establishing Dubai baseline living cost of AED 8,250 = €1,996 monthly).

Frequently Asked Questions: Methodology & Standards

How frequently is tax and social security data updated on Remotax?

Our statutory models are reviewed and updated continuously against official government budget laws and circulars. Each jurisdiction page clearly displays its last-verified date and direct links to official statutory sources.

What foreign exchange rates and valuation dates are used for cross-border comparisons?

Our calculation engine uses official European Central Bank reference exchange rates dated 2026-10-16: 1 EUR = 1.1253 USD, 1 EUR = 37.602 THB, and 1 EUR = 4.1327 AED (statutory peg of 3.6725 AED/USD). In Thailand, ฿38,500 converts to €1,024 monthly. In Dubai, AED 8,250 converts to €1,996 monthly.

Why does Remotax separate employee and contractor calculations?

Because statutory social security and allowable business expense rules diverge fundamentally between corporate employment and independent freelancing in almost every jurisdiction worldwide.

No. Remotax provides forward-looking simulations and benchmarks. Official tax returns must be prepared using certified local tax software or by licensed tax advisors.
